## Cookies
We set only **strictly necessary** authentication cookies (`sb-*-auth-token`) so you can stay
signed in. These are exempt from consent under the ePrivacy rules. We use no analytics or
marketing cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

## Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)
Our sign-in page uses **Cloudflare Turnstile** to tell humans from bots. Turnstile processes your
**IP address and browser/behavioural signals** to do this. This is a security measure based on our
legitimate interest in protecting the service. See Cloudflare's privacy documentation for details.

## Automated processing
Questline automatically calculates your XP, levels, and streaks from your activity. This is part of
the game mechanics and has **no legal or otherwise significant effect** on you. We do not carry out
automated decision-making in the sense of Art. 22 GDPR.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR you have the right to:
- **Access** your data — use **"Download my data"** in Settings to get a full JSON copy (Art. 15).
- **Portability** — that same export is machine-readable (Art. 20).
- **Rectification** — edit your name, habits, quests, sleep entries, and settings in the app (Art. 16).
- **Erasure** — use **"Delete account"** in Settings to permanently delete everything (Art. 17).
- **Withdraw consent** — disconnect Calendar, or **[turn off health-data processing]**, at any time,
  as easily as you gave it (Art. 7(3)).
- **Restriction / objection** — contact us at **[your email]** (Art. 18, 21).

To exercise any right that isn't self-service, email **[your email]**. We respond within one month.

## Health data
Sleep and weight are treated as **health-related (special-category) data**. We only process them
**with your explicit consent**, which you give the first time you enter them and can withdraw at any
time. If you withdraw consent, we stop processing this data and offer to delete what we hold.
